Our Distinctives

Rhythms of Prayer

We practice corporate, fixed-hour prayer practices that anchor us in worship, structure the day around Christ, and shape how we relate to time.

Life Together

We embody hospitality that invites relationship, strengthens community, and welcomes the wandering to build belonging in our everyday lives.

Thin Places

We embrace a sacramental view of reality that notices God's power and presence and is awake to the wonder of life on the land with Him.

Threshold Spaces

We are building bridges through low-risk exploration opportunities that cultivate curiosity and welcome questions.

Kindred Callings

We intentionally foster spiritual friendships which are marked by deep discernment and profound transformation to produce wise, relational leaders.

Anglican

We are an Anglican church rooted in historic Christian faith.
